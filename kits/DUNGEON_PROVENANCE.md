# Dungeon pack: where it comes from

Everything in `dungeon.glb` comes from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), published under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
(public domain, no attribution required; credit given anyway). Downloaded as glTF at 1k through `https://api.polyhaven.com/files/<id>`, converted with
`tools/scan/convert.mjs`, cut down with `tools/scan/slim.mjs` (WebP 512 px, welded, simplified, quantized). Used for the Old Mine: cave rock, roots, timbering,
the mine door, and the weapon tiers. Catalogue: `DUNGEON_CATALOG.json`.
