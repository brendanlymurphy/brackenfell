# Where the kit assets come from

Everything under `kits/` is public domain (CC0 1.0). Nothing here needs attribution; this file exists so the origin of every file can be checked.

## Model packs (Quaternius, free "Standard" tier)

| File | Source archive | Pieces |
|---|---|---|
| `village.glb` | https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/medieval_village_megakitstandard.zip (Medieval Village MegaKit) | 176 |
| `props.glb` | https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/fantasy_props_megakitstandard.zip (Fantasy Props MegaKit) | 95 |
| `nature.glb` | https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/stylized_nature_megakitstandard.zip (Stylized Nature MegaKit) | 68 |

Pack pages: https://quaternius.com/packs/medievalvillagemegakit.html, https://quaternius.com/packs/fantasypropsmegakit.html, https://quaternius.com/packs/stylizednaturemegakit.html

Licence text shipped in each archive (`License_Standard.txt`):

> License: CC0 1.0 Universal (CC0 1.0) Public Domain Dedication
> https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
> Models by @Quaternius

`tools/kits.mjs` converts the glTF files: every piece becomes one named node, materials and textures are shared, textures are resized to 1024 and stored as WebP, positions, normals and vertex colours are quantized, the second UV set and the chest's skin and animations are dropped (`Chest_Wood__Base` and `Chest_Wood__Lid` are separate pieces instead). `CATALOG.json` is generated by the same script.

Not converted: "Updated Modular Dungeon - May 2019" (https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/Updated%20Modular%20Dungeon%20-%20May%202019.zip, Quaternius, CC0). It ships OBJ, FBX and .blend only.

## Realistic skin (`kits/skin/`)

Built by `tools/skin.py`. Scanned texture sets are from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com, CC0), 1k JPG downloads via https://api.polyhaven.com/files/<id>:

| Kit material | Poly Haven asset | How it is used |
|---|---|---|
| `MI_Plaster` | `painted_plaster_wall` | replaces the painted texture (warmed and brightened) |
| `MI_UnevenBrick` | `old_stone_wall` | replaces the painted texture |
| `MI_Brick` | `medieval_blocks_03` | replaces the painted texture |
| `MI_RedBrick` | `medieval_red_brick` | replaces the painted texture |
| `MI_WoodTrim`, `MI_WoodTrim_Wear`, `MI_Trim_Furniture`, `MI_Trim_Props` | `wood_table_worn` | wood regions of the painted trim sheet are re-made from the scan, modulated by the painted sheet's edges and wear |

The other trim sheets (`MI_RockTrim`, `MI_RoundTiles`, `MI_MetalOrnaments`, `MI_Trim_Metal`, `MI_Trim_Cloth`) are the kit's own textures, desaturated and darkened, with grit from ambientCG `Rock020` (https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Rock020, CC0) mixed in.
Downloaded but not used in the end: `stone_wall`, `clay_roof_tiles_02`, `dark_wooden_planks`, `weathered_brown_planks`, `plastered_wall_04`, `castle_wall_slates`.
